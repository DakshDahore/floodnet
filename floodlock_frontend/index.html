QUESTION 01 // DEFINITION

What are urban floods?

Urban floods occur when extreme precipitation collides with impervious concrete surfaces, overwhelming stormwater drainage conduits and encountering locked gravity-fed ocean outfalls during high astronomical tides.

QUESTION 02 // HISTORICAL AUDIT

Why do I need to monitor them?

Urban flash inundations claim thousands of lives and cost billions in infrastructure destruction every year. Traditional municipal weather stations update once every few hours—failing to capture hyper-localized convective cloudbursts that submerge streets in under 20 minutes.

  • Global Catastrophic Flood Events: Tokyo Typhoon Hagibis, Mumbai July Monsoon Inundation, Germany Ahrtal Valley flash floods, and Mississippi River Delta surges: extreme storm rainfall overloading natural channels and urban drainage in under 30 minutes.
  • Severe Urban Surface Runoff: Concrete imperviousness transforms arterial transportation corridors into 2-meter deep canals within 20 minutes across global megacities.
