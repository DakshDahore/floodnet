QUESTION 01

What are urban floods?

Urban floods occur when extreme precipitation collides with impervious city concrete, overwhelming artificial stormwater conduits and locking gravity-fed ocean outfalls during high astronomical tides.

QUESTION 02

Why do I need to monitor them?

Urban flash inundations cost thousands of lives and billions in infrastructure damage annually. Traditional municipal weather stations update once every few hours—failing to capture hyper-localized convective cloudbursts that submerge streets in under 20 minutes.

QUESTION 03

How does FloodLock solve this?

Our 3-stage pipeline ingests live atmospheric instability metrics (CAPE, Soil Moisture, Tide), routes them through a custom temporal neural network running in milliseconds, and uses Google Gemini to generate actionable evacuation and municipal sluice-gate advisories.

QUESTION 04

Why is AI revolutionary here?

Standard computational fluid dynamics (CFD) require 14 hours on supercomputers to simulate a single storm surge. FloodLock's trained neural network predicts water depth and backflow resistance across thousands of city blocks in 12 milliseconds.
